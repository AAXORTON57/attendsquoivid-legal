This page describes the intended handling of information by AttendsQuoiVid, a tool for creating and managing short educational videos. Before public launch, the operator must confirm this description against the actual implementation and complete the disclosures below.

1. Information processed

When authorized by a TikTok account holder, the application may process TikTok account identifiers, profile information necessary for publishing, authorization tokens, granted permissions, video files, captions, publication settings, publication status, and technical error logs.

2. Why information is processed

Information is used to authenticate authorized accounts, prepare and transfer videos, check publication status, troubleshoot failures, and secure the service. The application does not request a TikTok password.

3. Sharing and processors

Publishing information and video content are shared with TikTok when a transfer or publication is authorized. Cloud hosting, storage, and automation providers may also process information when used. The actual providers and any international transfers must be documented before launch.

4. Storage and retention

Access tokens should be protected and limited to authorized processes. Video files and logs should be kept only for defined operational needs. Actual retention periods and security measures must be verified and specified before launch.

You may revoke TikTok authorization through TikTok's settings. For requests concerning access, correction, or deletion of personal data processed by AttendsQuoiVid, email abrunolg@gmail.com. Applicable rights depend on your jurisdiction.
